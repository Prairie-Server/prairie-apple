# Prairie Trademark & Brand Policy

Prairie is a fork of Prairie. Prairie's source code is free and open source under
the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later**
(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). You are welcome to use, study, modify, and redistribute
the code under those terms.

**That freedom applies to the code, not to the Prairie name and brand.** The
Prairie marks identify this project and its builds. This policy explains how
they may and may not be used so users can distinguish Prairie from other forks
and from the original Prairie project.

## Marks covered

The following Prairie marks are reserved, whether or not registered:

- the names **Prairie** and **Prairie Server**
- the **Prairie logo**, **wordmark**, and app icons
- Prairie product and component names, including **PrairieTV**,
  **PrairieMac**, and other "Prairie..." names
- confusingly similar variations of the above

The AGPL license over the source code **does not grant any license to these
marks.** This reservation is expressly permitted by AGPL-3.0 §7(e).

## Uses that are always fine

- **Referring to Prairie truthfully** - for example, "compatible with Prairie"
  or "a plugin for Prairie." Use the word to refer to this project, not to
  brand yours.
- **Referring to the fork history truthfully** - for example, "Prairie is a
  fork of Prairie" or "based on Prairie."
- **Redistributing official, unmodified Prairie builds** under the Prairie
  name, with all notices kept intact.
- **Running, modifying, and self-hosting** Prairie for yourself or your
  organization.
- **Editorial and educational** use - articles, tutorials, talks, and reviews.

## Uses that require written permission

- Distributing a **modified version under the Prairie name** (see *Forks*
  below).
- **Publishing an app to any app store** - Apple App Store, Google Play,
  F-Droid, or similar - under the Prairie name, logo, or a confusingly similar
  identity.
- Using the marks in a company, product, domain, or app name, or in any way
  that implies endorsement by the Prairie project.
- Merchandise or other commercial use of the logo, wordmark, or app icons.

## Forks must rebrand

You may fork Prairie under the AGPL. A **distributed** fork must not use the
Prairie name, logo, wordmark, or app icons as its identity. Pick your own name
and logo, remove the Prairie brand assets, and you may state factually that your
project "is based on Prairie" or "is a fork of Prairie."

## Brand assets are not under the AGPL

The Prairie logo, wordmark, app icons, and other visual identity files are
reserved brand assets and are **not** licensed under the AGPL or any other
open-source license. Any redistribution or fork must **remove or replace** them.
See the `NOTICE` file in the brand-asset directories.

Any app-store listing, download, or "Prairie" app that does not originate from
the official project is not authorized to use the Prairie marks.
